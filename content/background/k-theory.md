## K-theoretic Thom polynomials

There are three different versions of the concept ``K-theoretic fundamental class'':<ul><li>The class of the structure sheaf.</li><li>The class of the structure sheaf of a resolution, pushed forward.</li><li>The Motivic Chern class mC_y, with y=0 substitution.</li></ul>These three concepts only coincide if the variety has mild singularities.

Correspondingly, there are three different concepts for ``K-theoretic Thom polynomial''.

## On the portal

Two of the three concepts are recorded, both for the closure of the singularity and both written in double stable
Grothendieck polynomials $G_\lambda$ (see the [Conventions](conventions.md#k-theory)):

* the **push-forward version**: the class of the structure sheaf of a resolution, pushed forward;
* the **MS<sub>0</sub> version**: the motivic Chern class at $y=0$, which is the coefficient of $z^0$ of the
  [motivic Segre Thom polynomial](motivic.md) of the closure. It is listed separately where it differs from the
  push-forward version.

The class of the structure sheaf of the closure itself is not recorded.

The entries are on the pages of the singularities and in the [K-theory tables](../tables/ktp.md).
